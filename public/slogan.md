## About

Bin-Bandit is a waste-identifying system that scans the waste presented to it and guides it into the right bin.

## Waste categories

| Bin | Category |
|-----|----------|
| Green | Biodegradable |
| Blue | Non-biodegradable |
| Red | Residual / Special waste |
